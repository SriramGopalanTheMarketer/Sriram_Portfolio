03
B2B SAAS · SEO & GTM

Helped 13 bus operators stop paying OTA commissions.

THE PROBLEM

Bitla's clients — private bus operators — were listed on every major OTA but losing margin on every booking. They had their own apps and websites but no one was finding them organically. Every booking through an OTA was a commission paid out. The goal — drive direct bookings instead.

WHAT I DID

Handled SEO strategy for 13 top operators — building organic visibility for routes, destinations and brand searches. Set up complete GTM and GA4 infrastructure from scratch to measure actual user behaviour on their portals for the first time. No guesswork. Pure data.

AFTER Direct Bookings
